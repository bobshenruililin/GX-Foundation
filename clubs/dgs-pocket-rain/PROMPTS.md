# Pocket Rain artwork

The published story already has an illustration in every slot. These are optional art directions to paste into an image tool by hand. Use one per generation. Full-resolution watercolor masters live at `source/dgs-pocket-rain/artwork/` in the repository. The website serves the smaller WebP copies under `clubs/dgs-pocket-rain/assets/`.

## The two cups — assets/stall.webp

Use the attached stall photo as your composition reference. At cup height, paint two brown paper cups on a Hong Kong school-fair table. Keep the handwritten labels exactly “Filtered” and “Tap water”. Both cups hold clear water. Behind them, a single lopsided blue crochet drop peeks over the tinsel-trimmed box, just high enough for its two black bead eyes to show. Let us discover it on the second look. Delicate graphite and translucent watercolor on warm ivory paper, quiet blue and sage, soft paper margins, landscape 3:2. No other lettering.

## The clasp — assets/schoolbag.webp

Use the attached real blue plush as the character reference. Paint the moment a thumb and forefinger release its silver clasp onto the zip of a worn sage-green schoolbag. The little crochet drop has swung sideways and bumped against a pale blue refillable bottle. Keep its crooked tip, uneven yarn stitches and two black bead eyes. Leave the face mouthless. A strip of late afternoon light crosses the bag. Close, tender storybook watercolor on warm ivory paper, landscape 3:2, edges fading to paper, no lettering.

## The next person in line — assets/next-cup.webp

Paint a quiet close-up at a drinking-water station in Timor-Leste. A child steadies a small blue enamel cup under a simple metal spout. Behind it, an adult’s hand holds a cream jug with a chipped blue rim, waiting its turn. Show the last drop between the spout and the full cup. Keep the spout at 45% of image width and 62% of image height, with the cup rim just below at 67%, to leave room for an animated stream. Soft watercolor and graphite on warm ivory paper, natural gestures, sunlit greens, landscape 3:2. No lettering or logos.

The family scene is an imagined illustration. Keep GX’s reported machine count and projected water provision in the editable English and Traditional Chinese text. The interactive stream is positioned inside the picture’s 1536 × 1024 coordinate system.
